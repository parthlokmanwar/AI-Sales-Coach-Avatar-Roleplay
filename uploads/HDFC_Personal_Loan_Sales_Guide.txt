SECTION 2: WHO CAN APPLY — ELIGIBILITY QUICK CHECK

  SALARIED:
  ✓ Age: 21–60 years (at loan maturity)
  ✓ Min Net Salary: Rs. 25,000/month (Rs. 35,000 in metros)
  ✓ Work Experience: 2 years total | 1 year with current employer
  ✓ CIBIL Score: 700+ preferred (650+ considered case by case)

  SELF-EMPLOYED:
  ✓ Age: 25–65 years
  ✓ Min Annual Income: Rs. 2,50,000 (as per latest ITR)
  ✓ Business Vintage: 3+ years in same business
  ✓ CIBIL Score: 700+ required
  ✓ Business must show profit for 2 consecutive years

  PRE-APPROVED CUSTOMERS (Existing HDFC Customers):
  ✓ No separate eligibility check — instant approval
  ✓ Better rates — starts from 10.50%
  ✓ Minimal documentation
  ✓ Disbursed within 4 hours

SECTION 6: CUSTOMER OBJECTIONS & HOW TO HANDLE THEM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  OBJECTION 1: "Interest rate is too high"
  RESPONSE: "I understand your concern, sir/ma'am. Let me show you why many
  customers prefer HDFC even at this rate. First, the rate reduces on the
  outstanding balance — so effectively you pay less over time. Also, HDFC Bank
  offers complete transparency — no hidden charges, no surprises. For your
  profile, we can try to get the best possible rate after credit evaluation.
  Shall we proceed with checking your eligibility first?"

  OBJECTION 2: "I already have a loan with another bank"
  RESPONSE: "That's actually common with our customers. HDFC Bank allows you
  to take a Personal Loan even if you have an existing loan elsewhere, as long
  as your total EMI obligations don't exceed 50% of your monthly income — which
  is called FOIR. Let me quickly calculate whether you're within the limit."

  OBJECTION 3: "Processing fee seems high"
  RESPONSE: "The processing fee is a one-time charge — up to 2.5% + GST — and
  it covers documentation, credit assessment, and loan processing. On a loan of
  Rs. 5 lakhs, that's a maximum of Rs. 12,500. This is competitive in the
  market, and given HDFC's fast disbursal — within 4 hours for eligible
  customers — many find it worthwhile. We often have promotional offers that
  reduce or waive this fee — let me check what's available for you right now."

  OBJECTION 4: "I'll think about it / I need to compare with other banks"
  RESPONSE: "Absolutely, that's a wise approach. While you compare, let me
  highlight three things that make HDFC different: (1) Disbursal in 4 hours
  for existing customers — most banks take 2-5 days. (2) Dedicated relationship
  manager for your loan — you won't be left alone after application. (3) Easy
  foreclosure after 12 EMIs with just 2% charge. Take your time, but know that
  our current rates are valid until [month end] — shall I submit a pre-check
  so at least we know your eligibility before you decide?"

  OBJECTION 5: "I'm worried I won't be approved"
  RESPONSE: "That's a fair concern. The best way to know for certain is to run
  a soft check — this does NOT affect your CIBIL score and gives us a clear
  picture of your eligibility in minutes. You're under no obligation to take
  the loan after the check. Would that be okay?"

  OBJECTION 6: "I don't want my CIBIL score to be hit"
  RESPONSE: "Great question. The initial pre-check we do is a soft enquiry —
  it has zero impact on your CIBIL score. Only when you formally accept the
  loan and we process the disbursement does a hard enquiry appear. So your
  score is completely safe at the evaluation stage."

SECTION 7: CUSTOMER TYPES — HOW TO PITCH TO EACH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  YOUNG PROFESSIONAL (22-32 years, first job, city dweller):
  - Key need: Immediate cash for gadget/travel/rent deposit/wedding contribution
  - Pitch angle: Speed, convenience, app-based journey, no collateral
  - Key line: "No need to ask anyone for money — just your salary slip and we
    transfer funds within hours."

  MARRIED SALARIED (33-45 years, family responsibilities):
  - Key need: Home renovation, child's education, medical emergency
  - Pitch angle: Large amounts available, fixed EMI so budget is predictable
  - Key line: "Plan your expenses without disrupting your family's savings or
    breaking FDs — a fixed EMI fits perfectly into your monthly budget."

  BUSINESS OWNER / SELF-EMPLOYED (35-55 years):
  - Key need: Working capital, business expansion, bridge finance
  - Pitch angle: Fast disbursal, no asset pledging, can use for any purpose
  - Key line: "Keep your business assets free — use HDFC Personal Loan for
    short-term needs without touching your machinery or property."

  EXISTING HDFC CUSTOMER (any segment):
  - Key need: Already familiar with HDFC — just needs a nudge
  - Pitch angle: Pre-approved offer, best rates, minimal docs, instant disbursal
  - Key line: "You already have our trust — your pre-approved offer is waiting.
    The money can be in your account in under 4 hours."

SECTION 8: THINGS TO NEVER SAY TO A CUSTOMER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  ✗ "You will definitely get the loan" — never guarantee approval
  ✗ "Your CIBIL won't matter" — always be honest about credit requirements
  ✗ "We are cheaper than SBI/ICICI/Axis" — avoid direct rate comparisons
  ✗ "Processing fee can be waived if you pay me something" — STRICTLY PROHIBITED
  ✗ "Don't mention your existing loans in the application" — fraud/mis-selling
  ✗ "The charges are negligible, don't worry about them" — always disclose all fees
  ✗ Sharing customer's financial information with anyone outside the bank process

    system. No manual or offline applications are accepted.

  ★ KYC verification is MANDATORY before any loan is processed.

    charges, and terms before loan acceptance.

  ★ Customer grievance redressal: If customer is unsatisfied, direct them to
