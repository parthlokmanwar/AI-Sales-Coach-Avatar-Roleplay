RolePlay recording — does it actually work?

This is the exact mechanism used in the app, running standalone. No login, no server.
  1. Click Start recording.
  2. In the browser prompt, choose “This Tab” (or a window) and tick “Share tab audio”.
  3. Talk / play something, then click Stop & Save.
  4. The video downloads to your Downloads folder — and also plays back below so you can see it captured correctly.
